Capability · Operator reframing
Redesign incentives instead of forcing compliance
My family founded an education company with thousands of students. Long-running safety problem: kids ran around outside after class while waiting for pickup. Rules, regulation, scolding all failed. I inverted the frame — instead of forcing compliance, designed the option kids would choose. Built a play space with toys and TVs. Kids stayed willingly. Cheaper than more security; a multi-year problem dissolved.
