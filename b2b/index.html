I price for incentive-compatibility, not gut. Which AI pipelines get built first is a function of where the pricing structure says the leverage actually is.

How I design the system
Architecture-first when it matters. The two or three early calls — chunking strategy, cache layer, eval rubric, data model — decide everything downstream. I prefer the call that's defensible at scale to the one that's fastest to ship and rewrite.
